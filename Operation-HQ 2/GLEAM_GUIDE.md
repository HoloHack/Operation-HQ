# Gleam — Social Intelligence Lab

Gleam is a private app inside Operation HQ for building practical communication skill through explanation, rehearsal, low-pressure practice, and real-world evidence. It is not a personality score and does not claim to predict how another person will react.

## Open it

- Open **More → Gleam** from the bottom dock.
- Or press `Cmd/Ctrl+K` and search for **Gleam**.
- Or open Nexus and enter **open Gleam**.

Gleam does not occupy a default dashboard widget. It appears only when you deliberately open it.

## The practice loop

1. **Command** chooses an adaptive field mission from the least-practised skill among your selected focus tracks. It respects your chosen pressure level and avoids recent repeats when possible.
2. **Learn** contains 18 original micro-lessons across Conversation, Listening, Clarity, Assertiveness, Teamwork, and Speaking. Complete the knowledge check before recording a lesson.
3. **Simulate** contains eight offline branching situations with three decisions each. Feedback explains the visible cue behind each option. The result is cue alignment, not a judgement of personality.
4. **Missions** contains 18 real-world actions. Starting a mission does not count as completion. A real rep is recorded only after you add a factual reflection about what happened.
5. **Rehearse** builds a flexible conversation map: opening, two questions, message spine, reality check, and exit. It avoids fake dialogue and does not guess motives.
6. **Evidence** shows recorded practice by type and track, active days, field reps, and your own before/after reflection trend.
7. **Control** changes your daily target, mission pressure, and focus tracks. It also exports or erases only Gleam data.

## Metric definitions

| Signal | Exact meaning |
|---|---|
| Real reps | Field missions completed with a factual reflection |
| Practice sessions | Completed lessons, simulations, and saved rehearsals |
| Reflection trend | Average change between your own 1–5 before/after field-mission ratings |
| This week | Distinct local calendar days with a recorded practice action in the last seven days |
| Practice coverage | Recorded practice across six tracks, capped at five records per track for the visual ring |
| Cue alignment | How a simulation choice or draft matches the communication cues disclosed in the module |

These are practice records. They do not measure worth, popularity, personality, confidence as a trait, or how other people see you.

## Optional local coaching

The deterministic cue analyser works immediately and explains every cue it detects. **Deep coach · Local AI** becomes available only after you explicitly load Native Intelligence from Settings. The model runs in the new-tab session through WebGPU and receives only the text you submit to that action.

The local coach is constrained to practical, age-appropriate communication feedback. It must not diagnose personality or intent, predict reactions, or teach manipulation, coercion, deception, status games, romantic tactics, or scripted domination.

## Privacy and control

- No account or subscription.
- No contacts, microphone, camera, email, tabs, browsing history, or passive monitoring.
- No Gleam network request or cloud sync.
- Active mission reflections are saved locally while you type so section changes do not erase them.
- Export creates a Gleam-only JSON file.
- Erase removes the Gleam profile, lessons, attempts, missions, rehearsals, and reflections without touching the rest of Operation HQ.

## Recommended first session

Choose two or three focus tracks, set **Light pressure**, complete one lesson, run the matching simulator once, and select one field mission that genuinely fits the day. Keep the reflection short and factual; no names or private details are needed.
